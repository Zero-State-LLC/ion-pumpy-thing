    Where this comes from. The physics, numbers and quotations are drawn from The Vanishing, Part Three: The Science (corrected edition, November 2026), The MH370 Videos, Measured and Fusion Energy on 4orbs.com. Lines marked "our sum" are this game's own arithmetic. The simulations are simplified teaching models, not engineering.

    What this is about. The two videos are claimed to show Malaysia Airlines Flight 370, which disappeared on 8 March 2014 with 239 people on board. The satellite data points to the southern Indian Ocean, three pieces of debris trace to the aircraft, and the usual reading of the videos is that they are visual effects. 4orbs argues they are more likely real than not. This game doesn't settle that. It shows what would have to be true.
