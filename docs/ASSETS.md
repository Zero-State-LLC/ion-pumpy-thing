## Notes

- three.js 0.187.0 publishes no minified build, so the files ship as published; the host compresses them in transit.
- The airliner is a prop (see `GLOSSARY.md`): it brings shape only, and makes no claim of its own. It is a generic twin-engine wide-body with no livery, logo, text, registration or other marking. It has 6,076 triangles, no textures and no materials. Meshy's preview drew the span about 4 % short of the length, so the cleaning script scales the span and the length separately to their real sizes.
- To rebuild `assets/airliner.glb`: download the preview mesh of the task above while Meshy keeps it, check its hash, and run `python tools/clean-airliner.py preview.glb assets/airliner.glb` (Python 3 with numpy). The output's hash should match the table.
- The plate mount is a prop: shape only. It is a round base, a post holder and a post, plain metal with no text, logo or marking, 3,111 triangles, no textures and no materials, about 110 mm across. Meshy drew the post rising from a holder block rather than the slotted clamp the prompt asked for, so the Open step's plates in 3D rest on the post's tip, as a lab's post-mounted part does. The plates, their gap, the throat and the plasma bubble are drawn by the game from the step's numbers, not from props.
- To rebuild `assets/plate-mount.glb`: download the preview mesh of the task above while Meshy keeps it, check its hash, and run `python tools/clean-plate-mount.py preview.glb assets/plate-mount.glb` (Python 3 with numpy). The output's hash should match the table.
- The vacuum chamber is a prop: shape only. It is a ribbed drum with a flanged, bolted lid and floor, plain metal with no text, logo or marking, 4,214 triangles, no textures and no materials. Meshy drew it about as tall as it is wide; the Hold step sizes its side wall to the flat view's walls and its height to theirs, so the game draws it a little squatter than the file. The 3D view cuts away its near half, so the plasma ball and coils inside show. The ball, its cusp leaks, its particles and the coils are drawn by the game from the step's numbers, not from props. Two earlier previews for this prop (tasks `01a20212-a1d3-720d-abf2-cebe803cc234` and `01a20218-3840-73ed-9aab-13ba2474001b`) were not used: one was twice as tall as wide, the other added a gauge, a window and legs.
- To rebuild `assets/vacuum-chamber.glb`: download the preview mesh of the task above while Meshy keeps it, check its hash, and run `python tools/clean-vacuum-chamber.py preview.glb assets/vacuum-chamber.glb` (Python 3 with numpy). The output's hash should match the table.
- The piston housing and pump handle are props: shape only. The housing is a flanged barrel with a capped top, the handle a T with a short stem, a small hub and a threaded tip; both plain metal with no text, logo or marking, no textures and no materials, 3,193 and 1,663 triangles. The Ignite step sizes the housing's wall and height to the flat view's barrel, so the game draws it narrower and taller than the file, and cuts away its near half, so the piston inside shows. The piston, its travel, the pipe, the jar, the plasma's glow, its front and its particles are drawn by the game from the step's numbers, not from props.
- To rebuild `assets/piston-housing.glb` and `assets/pump-handle.glb`: download the preview mesh of each task above while Meshy keeps it, check its hash, and run `python tools/clean-pump.py housing preview.glb assets/piston-housing.glb` or `python tools/clean-pump.py handle preview.glb assets/pump-handle.glb` (Python 3 with numpy). Each output's hash should match the table.
