Flip looks with the bar at the bottom or the arrow keys. The airliner is a placeholder built from simple shapes at 63.7 m long and 60.9 m across the wings; Meshy makes the real one once a look is picked.

Camera views are reconstructions in synthetic colour, as in the game. The sequence runs on the game's clock: one second orbiting, closing in at a tenth of the triangle per second, opening at a quarter smaller, then the 1.6 s flash.
