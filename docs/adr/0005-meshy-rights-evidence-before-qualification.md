# Meshy rights evidence before asset qualification

Status: accepted 22 November 2026 (PT)

Five 3D GLBs are listed in [the asset register](../ASSETS.md) as Meshy-generated models with paid-plan ownership claims. The owner decided that the asset-rights qualification cannot be marked complete until dated evidence ties **each exact Meshy generation task ID** to its generating account, applicable plan and license terms at the time, input-material rights, and any Meshy Community publication or license setting. The task ID and output SHA-256 must match the shipped asset. Keep detailed receipts private and record a sanitized evidence reference plus review date in the asset register; never store credentials or sensitive invoices in Git.

The alternative, trusting the register's paid-plan assertion without account evidence, was rejected because public terms cannot prove a specific customer's generation-time entitlement. Unverified assets remain a documented qualification gap, not proof of infringement or an instruction to roll back a previously shipped release. If adequate rights evidence is unavailable, ask the owner to approve attribution, regeneration, replacement or another supported licensing path rather than silently choosing one.
